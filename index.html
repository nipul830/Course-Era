FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Firm FAQ

What is Aura Farming?

Aura Farming is a simulated prop-trading environment focused on risk management and trading performance.

Is trading real?

No. The trading environment and account balances are simulated and do not execute real brokerage trades.

What are the challenge rules?

Challenge rules cover areas such as daily drawdown, maximum drawdown, profit targets and trade history.

How do I access my account?

Use the account access option above to access the trader portal with your existing account.
