Data & Account Security

Authentication is handled through Firebase. We protect account and profile information using authenticated access controls and limit account data to the platform functions that require it. Personal information is not intended to be publicly exposed through the trading interface. Access to account data should be treated as private and limited to the account holder and authorized platform operations.
